# 1.5: Parts of Speech

**Parts of Speech**

English has nine parts of speech.  Every word in English is at least one part of speech.  Many words can be more than one part of speech.  To understand English grammar, students need to gain an understanding of the parts of speech and what their function in the language is.  Below I will go over the various parts of speech and give you examples of each.
