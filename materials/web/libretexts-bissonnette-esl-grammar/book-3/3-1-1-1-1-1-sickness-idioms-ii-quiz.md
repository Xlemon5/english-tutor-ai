# 3.1.1.1.1.1: Sickness Idioms II Quiz

**Sickness Idioms II Quiz**

1.  The old man felt fit despite his age.

       a. sad          b. in good condition        c. faint        d. lousy

2. He had to watch his diet so that he didn't consume too much salt.

        a. be careful of the food he ate        b. go on a diet

c. lose weight                                 d. eat salty food

3. The constipated man was dreaming of moving his bowels.

       a. passing out             b. feeling fit       c. keeping food down      d. going to the bathroom

4. He felt lousy all day Sunday and finally made an appointment to go to see the doctor.

       a. in the pink      b. blue     c. faint               d. sick all over

5. His bad sickness finally ran its course and now he feels much better.

a. finished                       b. took a deep breath

c. threw up                      d. came down with something

6. The doctor told him to take a deep breath and hold it during the physical examination.

       a. say ah                        b. give a throat culture

       c pass out                       d.  breathe in, but not breathe out

7. The young Chinese girl really had the blues after she heard the news from her father about her mother’s sickness.

       a. felt in the pink              b. was depressed and sad

       c. moved her bowels         d. broke out in a cold sweat

8. The poor man continuously had the chills for ten days.

a. watched his weight     b felt cold due to cold weather

c. was in the pink            d. felt cold and sweaty at the same time

9. The shy young boy broke out in a cold sweat when the girl of his dreams suddenly came up to him and started talking to him.

        a. began to sweet because it was hot outside             b. ran his course

c. passed out                                                          d. started to sweat because he was nervous

10. The poor young bridegroom simply couldn't keep his food down.

       a. kept throwing up        b. moved his bowels              c. felt fit         d. was in the pink

11. He passed out on the living room floor and looked dead to the world within minutes.

       a. defecated          b. moved his bowels              c. watched his diet          d. fainted

12. The new singer that I saw in the nightclub last night can really sing the blues.

        a. sing popular songs                     b. sing sad songs

c. sing songs in a blue dress           d. sing and look bluish

13.  My friend has to watch his diet because he has sugar diabetes.

       a. be careful about what he eats      b.  eat a lot of sugar

       c.  look at food labels to make sure they contained sugar

       d.  eat anything he wanted to eat
