# 4.3.1.1.1.1.1: Present Tense Passive Exercise

**Present Tense Passive Exercise**

**Exercise 3:  Change the following sentences from the active voice to the passive voice, please.**

1.  We cook our dinner in the kitchen every evening.

2.  Many people burn wood in fireplaces during winter.

3.  My sons play the piano quite often in our house.

4.  I fix broken things in my house all of the time.

5.  The Oak Tree Cinema shows movies every day.

6.  Housekeepers make beds in motels seven days a week.

7.  Gardeners grow vegetables and flowers in gardens.

8.  People take medicine when sick.

9.  Pilots fly planes out of Seatac Airport twenty-four hours a day.

10.  Teachers correct examinations all over the world.

11.  Students learn foreign languages in classrooms all over the world.

12.  People drink water with meals in many countries.
