# English Grammar / Phrases and Clauses / Prepositional Phrases

**PREPOSITIONAL PHRASES**

Prepositional phrases are groups of words, such as *out of* and *on top of*.

a) A big truck parked in front of their car.

b) The cat jumped on top of the cupboard.

c) One girl sits in the middle of the playground and the others dance round her.

In the above examples the underlined parts are prepositional phrases.
